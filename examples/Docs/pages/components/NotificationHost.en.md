# NotificationHost

The notifications of the Application drawn by ElemenxUI (Native.Notify with Look elemenx): cards of Items ([{ID, Title, Text, Icon, Image, Variant, Actions: [{ID, Label}]}]), the newest at the bottom, in the window by the corner of the screen of a program or in the corner of the page on the web (Page). Choose(item, action) is called with a press on a card (action null) or on one of its buttons, Close(item) with its close button. The theme draws them: the elemenx-notification classes and the tokens of the surface.

## Properties

| Property | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `Items` | `[]` |
| `Page` | `false` |
| `Choose` | `null` |
| `Close` | `null` |
