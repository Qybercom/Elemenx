# DesignerStyle

The layout of the selected element of a designer of pages (the Designer Service), what every element has whatever its kind: its margin and its padding on a box of the box model, a field for each side (12 gives 12px; the four sides become one shorthand: 8px, 8px 16px...), its width and height with their least and greatest, and in a flex container the direction, the wrap, the alignment of the items across (align-items) and along (justify-content) as buttons with icons, and the gap. A choice pressed again is taken away. Every change goes to the Designer at once (SetStyle).
