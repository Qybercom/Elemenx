# BlueprintPreview

What a graph of Blueprints that makes a thing of data gives, drawn flat as it is changed: a cloud (CloudBlueprints) its points, turned round its height (a drag turns it; Turn and Pitch in radians), each of its color and brightness, larger the larger it is; one of no places its fields (Distance and Anomaly round a middle, else its first two across and up); a network (NetworkBlueprints) its ways, each of a color of its class, wider the more it carries, on a globe turned as a cloud is, on a ground seen from over it (Inputs: what it is given, else a sample of each of its inputs); a layout (LayoutBlueprints) its streets, walls, roads and ramps and its lots and houses of a sample, seen from over it (Drawing); an image (ImageBlueprints) its picture, as large as it fits. Graph is the graph (an object, its JSON, or the name of a resource), Parameters what it is run with, Height its height in pixels (it is as wide as its place); Label names it for screen readers. A graph that cannot be made tells why. What it drew is told: drawn ({Kind, Count}).
