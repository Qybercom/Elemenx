# SceneBlueprints

SceneBlueprints: what a Scene adds to Blueprints, declared in $Provides: Blueprints makes it as a graph of an effect or one of its blocks is met, so a Scene calls nothing first.

- The kind effect: a graph of the layers of an effect of a scene, ending at Effect (it takes the blocks of a generator too). Make(parameters) of what Compile gives is the type of the effect. Its blocks: Layer (each of a Kind, its numbers as inputs a Parameter can take, else its properties; each one after the layers under it) and Effect, the end (its lamp, heat, aim and flight, the layers given).
- The blocks of the passes of the picture of a render graph (of BlueprintShaders): Scene, Clouds, Occlusion, Air, Frames, Mirror, Rays, Exposure, Bokeh, Bloom, Picture and Shader; each one runs the step of the scene that runs it (frame.Steps).
- The kind water: the water of a Scene as a graph (blueprintWater in its shader of water), its blocks the parts of the water of the library (Waters).
- The kind star: the look of a star at a time, run each frame (it takes the blocks of a generator too: Time, Curve, Parameter...); Look(parameters, seconds) of what Compile gives is {Corona, Prominences, Flares, Count, Height, Speed, Strands, Tint}. Its end, Star, takes each as an input (its property when nothing comes): a Curve of the Time makes its prominences rise and fall as the keys say.
- The kind nebula: the look of a nebula at a time, run each frame as the one of a star; Look(parameters, seconds) is {Glow, Density, Turn, Grow, Detail, Outer, Inner, Turbulence, Thickness, Hollow}: how bright, how thick its dust, how far it is turned round its axis (degrees), how much larger than its Radius (a shell that spreads), how much fine grain it shows near, the tints of its outer and inner colors; Turbulence, Thickness and Hollow are of its shape (how torn, how thick its shell, how wide the hollow in its heart), taken at its time 0 (its shape is made once of them).
- The kind bake: the surface of a body of noise (not of ScenePlanet) baked into its two cubes, blueprintBake(p, inout surface, inout sky) of the shader Bake of a Scene (BakeGraph): surface its ground (rgb) and gloss (a), sky its clouds (r), glow (g) and sparks (b). Its inputs are Input blocks (PX, PY, PZ: the direction; Seed, Ocean, Cratered, Kind, Ice, Clouds), its numbers the blocks of a generator, so what comes to BakeLand (Land, H) is read in the page too: BakeLandGraph(graph) that part of it as a generator (its result Land), the ground of the body (SceneTerrainGenerator.Sphere) the very land its picture shows. Its other blocks: BakeRocky, BakeIcy, BakeLava, BakeGas (the surface of each kind: rgb and gloss), BakeKind (the one of the kind of the body), BakeIce (its caps), BakeClouds and BakeEnd.
- The kind weather: what the weather and the year do to the surface of a thing, the functions its shaders (Pbr, Solid) call (WeatherGraph of a Scene): WeatherSeason (weather_season(inout color, tree, at, turn): the leaves turn, fall, blossom), WeatherSnow (weather_snow_<For>(up, at): how much snow lies, For things, solid or plants), WeatherDissolve (weather_dissolve(world, inout color): a body beamed away), WeatherPuddles (weather_puddles(wet, level, at): water standing), WeatherEnd (they all come to it). Their numbers (thresholds, scales of their noise) are their properties.
- The kind air: the air between the eye and what it sees, functions of the shaders (AirGraph of a Scene), each block of them for those that ask (#define ENX_AIR_<FOR> before the place of the graph): AirHaze (air_haze_<For>(world), how much of it, air_horizon_<For>(light), its color; For globe: the things and the ground of a globe, ground: the land and the water of a scene), AirFog (the pass Air: air_fog(place, far, way) of a fog that lies low and of the sheets of rain, air_light(whiten), air_far(way), air_veil(ray)), AirUnder (air_under(color, place, depth): the eye under the sea), AirEnd. Their numbers are their properties. Chunk(context, name, lines, guard): the function of a block a part of the shaders of Renderer (its name of its text), asked by the block; guard: the name it stands under (#ifdef), or names, any of them (#if defined).
- The kind clouds: the clouds of the shaders (CloudsGraph of a Scene), each block of them for those that ask (#define ENX_CLOUDS_<FOR>): CloudCover (clouds_cover: the cover of the weather of a planet at a place, its billows), CloudGlobe (cloudSeen, cloudAt: the globe and the shadows of its clouds), CloudShell (shellThick: round the eye over a surface), CloudLayer (lobe, thick: the layer of a scene, of a cheap 3D noise), CloudsEnd.
- The weather of a planet as blocks of numbers of a generator (SceneForecast, its Kernel: the same in the page and in a shader): ForecastStorms, ForecastFronts, ForecastHeaps, ForecastCirrus, ForecastRain, ForecastShroud; LibraryForecast() the graph of them of the library (blueprints/shader/forecast), the WEATHER of a Scene (ForecastGraph).
- The arms of a galaxy as blocks of numbers of a generator (GalaxyData.Kernel: the page and a shader alike): GalaxyTurn, GalaxyBend, GalaxyWave, GalaxyArm; LibraryGalaxy() the graph of them of the library (blueprints/shader/galaxy), Galaxy(graph) {Of, Arm, GLSL (vec4 galaxy_spiral(...))}: its stars, its picture, the band of it from a system alike.
- The kind cosmos: the sky far behind a system (CosmosGraph of a Scene), its blocks the parts of that sky: see Cosmoses; LibraryCosmos() the graph of the library (blueprints/shader/cosmos).
- The kind ring: a ring round a body (RingGraph of a Scene), its bands, its gaps, its colors and its light as blocks: see Rings; LibraryRing() the graph of the library (blueprints/shader/ring).
- The kind planet: a globe from far (PlanetGraph of a Scene): its picture lit, its clouds and their shadows, the shadow of its ring, its towns at night, its air at its edge as blocks: see Planets; LibraryPlanet() the graph of the library (blueprints/shader/planet). Painted(kind, end, head): such a kind of a function of a body in a shader.
- The kind ground: the ground of a globe near and from over it (GroundGraph of a Scene): its picture, woods, fields, snow, dunes, relief, the tiles near, its light and the shadows of clouds and of its ring, water, towns at night, clouds from over them and air as blocks: see Grounds; LibraryGround() the graph of the library (blueprints/shader/ground).
- LibraryShader(name): a shader graph of the library (blueprints/shader/<name>) read; LibraryCosmos(), LibraryRing(), LibraryPlanet(), LibraryGround(), LibraryForecast(), LibraryBolt() and LibraryGalaxy() each give theirs of it.
- The shape of a lightning, one for the bolt of a storm (SceneLighting) and the one of an effect (SceneEffects, kind bolt): a generator of the jag of each of its steps (Step, Seed: X and Z, -0.5 to 0.5), its parameters Steps (of its way), Wide (a jag to its length), Fork (the step a branch leaves at), ForkSteps, ForkDown and ForkWide (the branch: its steps, how far down and how wide each, to the bolt). LibraryBolt() the one of the library (blueprints/shader/bolt); Bolt(graph) {Jag(step, seed) ([X, Z]), Steps.. ForkWide, GLSL (the text of vec2 bolt_jag(float step, float seed), the shape in a shader: of a hash of a sine, its floats part from the numbers of the page where the sine is large, the way the same)}, made once a graph.
