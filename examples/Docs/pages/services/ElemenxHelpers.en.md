# ElemenxHelpers

The helpers the Components and the Services of ElemenxUI share, so each is written once: the text of a body without the indentation of its template (BodyText), the first day of a week (FirstDay), and a list of listeners that tells each of them apart (Listeners). An Application may use them too; its own Services keep their names.
