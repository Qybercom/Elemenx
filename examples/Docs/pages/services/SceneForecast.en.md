# SceneForecast

SceneForecast: the weather of a planet over its body, of one source in the kernel language of Elemenx.Compilers (Kernel, on the value noise of the Kernel of the Noise Service): the same in the page and in a shader. A graph of numbers of Blueprints (blueprints/shader/forecast, the ForecastGraph of a Scene) is made of its blocks, so the shader that bakes the weather of a planet (WEATHER of Scene) and the page that asks what it is over the eye read the very same map.

- Its functions: wx_wind (the wind of a direction, radians a day along the ground: the jets of the belts, storms that turn the air round their eyes, eddies of a noise; and how strong a storm is there), wx_air (where the air was: fronts and streams of a few hundred km drawn out along the wind, the middle of them, the storm), wx_fronts (the sheets of a front, how humid the air is: its belts, its land, its climb up a range), wx_heaps (heaps of the heat of the day and the kind of the clouds), wx_cirrus, wx_rain, wx_shroud (cover, kind, cirrus and rain, a world of a shroud or of none).
- Code(): the Kernel as js (made in the page); IR(): its module of the IR, with the noise it calls (for Blueprints.Numeric).
- Air(p, days, seed, radiusKm, scale, fine, eddy) and the others: what the functions give, in the page.
