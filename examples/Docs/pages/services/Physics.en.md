# Physics

Physics: how things move, apart from what shows them. Nothing here knows a scene: a state goes in, with what is asked of it and how long a step is, and comes out moved. Steps are short and of one length, so a slow machine moves a thing as a fast one does.

- Steps(clock, elapsed, length, each): runs each(length) as many times as the time asks, keeping the rest in clock {Left}; returns how far into the next step the time has come, for Blend.
- Blend(from, to, part): a place between two, a number or a list.
- Spring(value, speed, target, stiffness, damping, elapsed): [value, speed] of a thing pulled to a target.
- Car(state, input, surface, settings, elapsed): input {Gas, Brake (and then back; true, or 0 to 1 as far as a trigger is pressed), Stop (all wheels, and it stays), Steer (-1 to 1, left over 0), Hand, Drift (the rear lets go while it is held)}; docs/VEHICLES.md says how all of it hangs together; surface(x, z) is {Height, Grip, Drag}. Settings: Mass, Power, Top, Brake, Base (from axle to axle), Width (between the wheels), Lock (the most the wheels turn, radians), Answer (how fast they turn, and the car after them), Keep (at what speed they have half their lock left), Turn (a table of how fast it turns at full steering: [[speed m/s, degrees a second], ...], instead of what Lock and Keep give; the ground still limits it, it needs Assist, and a drift goes by the tyres), Build ([at a standstill, at Top]: seconds the steering takes to reach full, so that a touch is a little turn; it comes back twice as fast), Bite (how far brakes go past the grip), Coast (what slows it off the gas, m/s²), Agility (how much harder than its grip alone it turns: above 1 a game has it sharper than a real car), Assist (0 a simulator: only the tyres turn it; 1 an arcade: it goes where the wheels point), Rear (the grip behind against the front), Slide (the grip behind that is left when it drifts), Angle (how far a drift may open, radians), Peak (the slip, radians, at which a tyre gives most), Grip, Downforce (how much grip the air adds at top speed, as a part of it), Ride (how soft the springs are), Travel (how far a wheel goes up or down), Balance (how high its weight lies), AntiRoll (the bars that hold it level in a turn, N m a radian), Gears with Shift, ShiftTime, Redline and Idle (a gearbox: Gearbox; with none, the engine gives its power at any speed). State: {X, Z, Heading, Forward, Aside, Rate, Steer, Y, Rise, Pitch, PitchSpeed, Roll, RollSpeed, Spin, Wheels, Strokes, Touching}. Force is what the engine gives at a speed, as far as the tyres that touch hold; air and the ground take it back. Each of the four wheels [front left, front right, rear left, rear right] is a weight of its own on a spring and a damper under the body, so it follows the road and the body rides over it, leaning as it speeds up, brakes and turns. Strokes is how far each spring has gone from rest, above 0 when the wheel hangs lower than it did.
- Gearbox(state, input, settings, gas, braking, speed, elapsed): the gears of a car (Car runs it when it has Gears): Gear, Rpm, Shifting, Shifted in the state; returns how much of its power the engine gives now.
- Hit(state, normal, depth, at, settings): a car at a wall: normal [x, z] looks out of the wall, depth is how far in it went, at [x, z] is the corner that met it, from its middle. It comes out, gives up its speed into the wall but for a bounce (Bounce, default 0.25), scrapes (Scrape, default 0.12), and turns round what it hit.
- Bounce(way, normal, depth, bounce): the same for a list [x, y, z]: returns the way after it.
- Ship(state, input, settings, elapsed): input {Yaw, Pitch, Roll, Throttle, Lift, Boost, Stop (all of it comes to rest)}. Settings: Top, Boost, Turn, Roll, Assist (how soon it gives up a way it no longer points). State: {At, Right, Up, Ahead, Way, Spin: [pitch, yaw, roll], Throttle}.
- Boat(state, input, sea, bed, settings, elapsed): input {Gas, Brake (and astern; true, or 0 to 1), Steer (-1 to 1, left over 0), Stop}; sea(x, z) is {Height} of the water there (SceneWaves.At), bed(x, z) the height of the ground under it. Settings: Power (m/s² the motor gives, 6), Top (m/s, 16), Turn (radians a second at speed, 0.9), Length (4.5), Width (1.8), Draft (how deep it lies, 0.35), Plane (how far its bow rises at speed, radians, 0.09), Lean (how far it leans into a turn, 0.12). State: {X, Z, Heading, Forward, Aside, Rate, Y, Rise, Pitch, PitchSpeed, Roll, RollSpeed, Aground}.
- World(settings): a world of solid bodies. Settings: Gravity (m/s² down, or a list [x, y, z]), Ground (a height, a function (x, z) of the height, or null for none; 0 by default), Friction, Bounce and Rolling of the ground, Step (1/120 s), Iterations (10), Sleep (false: none sleep), Touch(a, b, {At, Normal, Impulse}) (when two begin to touch; b is null for the ground). It has: - Add(body): body {ID, Shape ('sphere', 'box', 'capsule' standing along its y, 'prism': a convex outline Points [[x, z]...] round its middle standing Height high, that never moves; 'tube': a pipe that never moves, its axis Points [[x, y, z]...] from its At, Radius out to its outside, Wall (its thickness, a tenth of it): what is outside stays out of it, what is in it stays in, its ends open), Radius, Size [x, y, z] (a box), Height (a capsule, between the middles of its ends; a prism, all of it), Mass (0: it never moves), Static, At, Turn [x, y, z, w], Way, Spin (radians a second round each axis), Bounce, Friction, Rolling (what holds back its rolling: 0 rolls on for ever, 0.03 by default), Damping, Angular (how it slows by itself), Gravity (how much of it the body feels), Sleeping}; the world moves the body itself, so what shows it reads At and Turn, or blends from Was and WasTurn (where it was a step before) by what Step returns, for frames that come between steps; - Remove(body), Bodies(), Clear(), Step(elapsed) (returns how far into the next step the time has come, as Steps does); - Push(body, impulse, at) (an impulse [x, y, z], N s, at a point of the world, the middle when none), Wake(body); - Joint(a, b, {At, From, To, Length, Rope}): holds a point of a (From, or At) to a point of b, or of the world when b is null (To, or At), at a Length (what it is now by default; 0 is a ball joint): a rod, or a rope that only pulls; returns {Remove()}; - Ray(from, direction, max, filter(body, null for the ground)): the nearest {Body (null: the ground), At, Normal, Distance}, or null; - Contacts(): what touched in the last step, {A, B, At, Normal (out of B into A), Depth, Impulse}. - Ragdoll(at, {Height, Mass, Group}): a body of a person standing at a point, eleven parts on ball joints (Parts {Head, Chest, Hips, ThighLeft, ShinLeft, ArmLeft, ForearmLeft and the Right ones}), that falls limp; returns {Parts, Remove()}. Water ({Level: a height or a function (x, z), Density 1000, Drag 1}) floats what is lighter than the water it puts aside, a box on its face; a body's Group (any word) is one with the others of it: they never strike one another.
- Cloth({Columns, Rows, Spacing, At, Across, Down, Pinned (top, ends, corners, none, or the indices), Shear, Bend, Gravity, Iterations, Damping, Ground, Wind, Colliders [{At, Radius}]}): a grid of points held to their neighbours: {Columns, Rows, Points (row by row, each [x, y, z]), Links ([a, b, length]), Index(column, row), Step(elapsed), Pin(i, at), Release(i), Blow(wind)}.
- Rope({From, To, Segments, Pinned (start by default)} and those of Cloth): a cloth one point wide.
- Waves({Columns, Rows, Cell, At [x, z], Speed, Damping}): the face of water as a grid of heights: {Heights, Step(elapsed), Disturb(x, z, amount, radius), Height(x, z)}.
- Fluid({Radius (how far a particle feels the others), Density, Stiffness, Viscosity, Gravity, Min, Max (its box), Ground (a height or a function (x, z) of it: it lies on it and spreads over it), Friction (of the ground: the part of its speed along it it loses in a second, 0.3), Step (seconds, 1/120; longer is cheaper and coarser), MaxSpeed (m/s: no particle faster)}): water of particles: Add(at, way), Pour(at, count, across), Step(elapsed), Particles() ([{At, Way, Density, Age}]), Count(), Clear(), Keep(test) (only the particles test(particle, index) is true of stay: what soaked away, what ran off).
