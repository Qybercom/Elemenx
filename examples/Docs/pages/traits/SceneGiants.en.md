# SceneGiants

SceneGiants: the inside of a giant of gas or of ices, as its envelope has it (Envelope Service: Scene EnvelopeOf). The eye under the top of its decks of clouds (40 km over the highest of them) is in it: what it sees there is its air at that depth, over all that lies far (the sky, the stars, the giant from outside):

- its decks of clouds as they pass (billows of a noise round the eye, layered as the depth is, of the colour of the deck it is in), lit from above by as much of its sun as gets there (Light of the envelope, as an eye used to the dark sees it: to the power 0.4), on its day side, darker as it goes down;
- under them the dark, and the glow of the heat of the depth (Glow of the envelope: dark red past some 800 K, orange, yellow, white deep in its hydrogen made metal), from below, brighter as it goes down;
- in a deck of water (Lightning of its condensable, or its name of water) flashes of lightning in the clouds round it;
- in the seat of a ship (Person first of the Scene: scene.Inside) drops of its clouds on the glass;
- the light of the scene: its stars as much as their light gets there, the glow of the depth a light from the middle of the giant (engine.GiantLight, of Scene lights), a flash of lightning white over all.

A ship stops only at its core (engine.SolidOf of SceneVehicles: the Floor of its envelope), as fast deep in it as so high over it; while the eye is in it its globe is not drawn from outside. engine.Giant: what the eye is in ({Body, Height (km over its 1 bar), Fade (0 over its decks to 1 in them), State (Envelope At), Seen, Color, Opacity, Lightning, Up}) or null; engine.GiantNow() the same, once a frame; engine.GiantDraw(sky) draws it over what lies far (and the drops over all); engine.GiantStep(elapsed) its lightning. Seen in the demo Universe: Jove, a giant of the system of Terra.
