# SceneVehicles

Vehicles: what is driven. An object with Vehicle {Kind, ...} under the keys (Control) goes by its kind: a car on the ground, a ship in space. Their ways of moving are in Vehicles of the scene, by kind, and Vehicles({Kind: step}) adds more: step(hero, keys, elapsed). How they move is the Physics service, in steps of its own: what shows is a blend of the last two, so nothing shakes at any pace.

A car, besides the settings of Physics.Car: Yaw (the turn of its model, degrees), Heading, Seat ([x, y, z] of the eyes), Wheels (a word for what of its model, by the name of a node or of its material, is a wheel). A car drives on the roads of the scene (kind road): the grip and drag of what is under each wheel, a strip, a kerb, a junction or the ground. W gives gas, S brakes and goes back, A and D steer, Space is the brake, Shift lets the rear go for a drift, C is the hand brake. Wheels spin, the front ones steer, and each goes up and down on its spring.

A ship, besides those of Physics.Ship: Yaw, Facing ([x, y, z] it looks at first), Follow ([back, up] of the view, in its size), Around (the ID of the body it goes by and must stay out of), From ([x, y, z] from that body, in its size); in real sizes (Scale real of the Scene) Length (metres: how large it is), Speed (m/s, 3000, near the body) and Cruise (how many times faster far from it, 1000, from 60 000 km over it, as the root of how high it is) in place of Top. The keys fly it: A and D or the arrows turn it, R and F or the arrows up and down pitch it, Q and E roll, W and S set its speed, C and X lift it up and down, Shift boosts, Space brings all of it to rest. The mouse only looks round it. Its Thrust goes to what burns on it. H takes it into warp and J jumps (Travel(id, kind, options, callback)), with Warp and Jump of its Vehicle: {To, Length, Enter, Inside, Exit, Tint}.

A boat, besides the settings of Physics.Boat (Power, Top, Turn, Length, Width, Draft, Plane, Lean): Yaw, Heading, Seat. It floats on the sea of the ground, on its waves (SceneWaves), at its four ends; W gives it the motor, S goes astern, A and D turn the rudder, Space stops it; it runs aground where the bed comes up under it. It leaves foam behind it (the effect wake), more as it goes faster, and throws up water at its bow when it goes fast (spray). The model boat of SceneModels is one.

A car stays off the barriers of the roads by all its corners (Long and Wide: half of its length and width), and a vehicle of any kind meets what stands in its way: another vehicle, a craft, a body, a wall of a location.

Openings of a vehicle are what moves on it: {Name, Match (a word of the materials; with Nodes: true, of the nodes too), Low, High (the box of it in its model) or Disc ({At, Axis, Radius, Depth}: round an axis, as a steering wheel), Both (the same on the other side), Way ([x, y, z]), Reach, Open}, or, turning, Hinge ([x, y, z]), Axis, Angle (degrees at 1), Aside and AsideAngle (a second axis); a pair hinged on both sides turns each its own way, and so does what such a pair carries (Follow and Both: the glass of each door). A piece moves with one opening, the first that takes it. OpenPart(id, name, amount) moves it, by degrees, on a car or a ship (its hatches). They need not be parts of the file. What the driver moves has a Drive: steer, gas, brake, hand, gear (its Gates, {R, N, D, 1, 2...: [ahead, aside]}; an automatic's R, N, D and a manual's H by default), sequential, paddle, indicator, wipers (Period; they go while it rains, or as Wipers of the vehicle says); Sign 1 or -1 keeps one way of it, Follow names the opening it is carried by (a paddle on the wheel), Pace how fast it comes (a second). A car with Gears has a gearbox (Physics.Gearbox): Shift auto, or manual with E up and Q down.

Lamps of a car are its lights: {Kind (head, rear, brake, reverse, indicator), Match and Low, High, Disc, Nodes, Both as an opening has them (their glass, each side apart), At (where the light comes from on the left, mirrored; the middle of the glass if not told), Color, Glow (how bright the glass glows), Power, Radius, Spot, Aim}. L switches the lights (Lights of the vehicle: auto by default, on at dusk, at night and in rain or fog), Z and X the indicators, which go off as the wheel comes back, both the hazard lights. A wet road holds WetGrip (0.75) of its grip.

The roles of the parts of a model (ModelRoles, set in the tab Roles of a ModelEditor) are openings, lamps and wheels of their own on any object of the model, a vehicle or not (the door of a house, a lever, a fan, a lamp over a porch): the parts each names (pieces of it, or the triangles of one a brush painted) cut out as those of an opening are, moving as its Motion says (hinge, slide, spin, glow), after the role it Follows. Parts(id) lists those of an object, Part(id, role) is one: Get(), Set(value) (the scene emits part); what it is set to comes by degrees, at its Pace.

Mirrors and Screens of a vehicle are panes in its model, {At, Normal (where it faces), Up, Width, Height}: a mirror shows the world reflected by its plane (Every frames apart, 2, each on a frame of its own, as finely as the screen in the part of it it covers; Tint, Blur, Strength), in the shape of its glass where a model of a file has it there (the triangles on its plane, Depth off it, 0.03, facing as it faces), a pane otherwise. Seen from the seat it is set as a driver sets it, what it shows level; View 'camera' (by itself where the body of the car stands in the way of what it shows, a car with no rear window) shows what a camera behind the car sees, straight back; View 'mirror' never does. Seat ([x, y, z], of the vehicle by default) is where the eye looks into it from. A screen shows what an element of the page shows (Source: the data-screen of an element in the screens slot of the scene, drawn every Every seconds into Pixels [width, height]; Bright). Only those of the vehicle that is driven are drawn.

Mounts of a vehicle of any kind are what it is built of, each where its model says, in its size: {Kind, At: [x, y, z], Aim: [x, y, z], Size, On, Mouth} and Type with what an effect takes (an effect that burns on it; Twist, degrees round the aim, leans it; Mouth, [width, height] or a radius, in its model, is the opening it comes out of: it fills it with light, the jet is as wide as it is, and an engine lights what is round it as it burns), or Model and Turn ([x, y, z] degrees; a model that stands on it). Kind is free: engine goes with the thrust, and burns as an exhaust if no Type is told; a game names its weapons, shields and holds as it likes, and asks MountsOf(id, kind) where they stand now. Pop of a mount is a burst there when a car lets go of the gas at speed: true for backfire, or {Type, Size, Every} (the seconds at least between two).
