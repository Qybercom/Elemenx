# SceneLanding

Landing: a ship comes down to a body and goes up from it by itself, as what it can do (not as a state of the scene). A ship has two traits for it:

- Entry {Mode (landing; impact is for what falls, later), Pace (how many times faster than real the way through the air goes high up: the last kilometres are in real time, 30), Cruise (how many times its Top a ship goes on its way to the body, 40), Parking (km: the low orbit it goes up to, as a station's, 400), Exit (km over the ground where a location is left for the surface going up, 0.6)}.
- Flight {Airspeed (m/s, 160), Climb (m/s, 30), Hover (metres over the ground it waits at before it comes down, 40), Agility (how quickly it answers, 1)}: it flies in the air of a ground as a craft that can hang still.

Land(ship, {Body, Latitude, Longitude} or {Marker}) takes it down: from space it goes to where its way down begins (fast, Cruise), then over the surface of the body (in kilometres, the body as large as it is) along a great circle to the point: from its orbit a long arc through the air, hot, slowing (an orbital entry); from low an inclined approach, steeper near the ground. Over the point the location of it (SceneLocations of the planet of the body, or one of noise for a body of no planet) is made the ground of the scene, what is of space away, and the ship hangs at Hover, comes down straight and sets down. TakeOff(ship) lifts it as a craft that hangs, it goes forward and up, leaves the location at Exit, and climbs over the surface to its Parking orbit, where it is a ship of space again. A press of a key of flight takes it over at any time in the air of a location (A and D turn it, W and S its speed, C and X up and down, Shift faster); over the surface W and S make the way down faster or slower, A and D move the point aside. The view goes after it in the axes of the ship (as round a ship in space), lagging and shaking in the heat. A point of the sea has its water at level 0: the ship sets down on it. The sun of a location goes as its body turns; each marker of the command says whether it is day there now. The scene emits landing ({ID, Phase, Body, Latitude, Longitude, Altitude (km)}) at each phase: transfer, entry, approach, hover, touchdown, landed, lift, climb, ascent, orbit, manual; and location ({ID, Location, Body}) when one is made. The command Landing of the scene (a button while a ship is driven near a body it can come down on) lists the markers of the body (Markers of its Surface or of its Planet: [{ID, Name, Latitude, Longitude}]) and lets a point of it be picked.
