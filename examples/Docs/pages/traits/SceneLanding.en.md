# SceneLanding

Landing: a ship comes down to a body and goes up from it by itself, as what it can do (not as a state of the scene). A ship has two traits for it:

- Entry {Mode (landing; impact is for what falls, later), Pace (how many times faster than real the way through the air goes high up: the last kilometres are in real time, 30), Cruise (how many times its Top a ship goes on its way to the body, 40), Parking (km: the low orbit it goes up to, as a station's, 400), Exit (km over the ground where a location is left for the surface going up, 0.6)}.
- Flight {Airspeed (m/s, 160), Climb (m/s, 30), Hover (metres over the ground it waits at before it comes down, 40), Agility (how quickly it answers, 1)}: it flies in the air of a ground as a craft that can hang still.

Land(ship, {Body, Latitude, Longitude} or {Marker}) takes it down: from space along one curve from where it is, as fast as it goes, to the top of the air (120 km; 40 with no air) as far from the point as its glide reaches, as fast as the glide goes on; then in the air over the surface of the body (in kilometres, the body as large as it is), the same ship going on: a long glide high up (hot, slowing at most 0.12 km/s a second), steeper near the ground, heading for the point; never a stop, never a step. Flown by hand down to the top of the air, it comes into it the same way, the player flying on (W and S its throttle, R and F its nose, A and D turn it); the command Landing then takes it down from there, and a key of flight takes it over from its plan at any time. Low over the point the location of it (SceneLocations of the planet of the body, or one of noise for a body of no planet) is made the ground of the scene, what is of space away, and the ship goes on into it as fast as it came, hangs at Hover, comes down straight and sets down. TakeOff(ship) lifts it as a craft that hangs, it goes forward and up, leaves the location at Exit (flown up by hand too), and climbs through the air to its Parking orbit, where it is a ship of space again, as fast as it went. In the air of a location A and D turn it, W and S its speed, C and X up and down, Shift faster. The view is the one round a ship in space all the way (the mouse turns it, the wheel brings it near), shaking in the heat. A point of the sea has its water at level 0: the ship sets down on it. The sun of a location goes as its body turns; each marker of the command says whether it is day there now. A location of a planet goes on past its outline: flown over it (not on its way down to a point), the ship is in the location beside, made the ground then, the ship and the view at the very point of the planet they were (seamless); through a passage of the mode passage, behind a veil (Veiled of the scene, 0 to 1); the location ahead is made before it comes, and if it is not there yet the view darkens until it is (Scene: Prepare). Past the ground of a location the surface it came over is drawn as its horizon (the far ground of the body). The scene emits landing ({ID, Phase, Body, Latitude, Longitude, Altitude (km)}) at each phase: transfer, entry, approach, hover, touchdown, landed, lift, climb, ascent, orbit, manual; and location ({ID, Location, Body}) when one is made. ToOrbit(ship) takes a ship near a body to its parking orbit by one curve, and round it by itself (a key of flight lets it go); TakeOff ends on it too. The command To orbit of the scene does it (ShipOrbit), with a hint nearer than 2000 km over the body (ShipHint: orbit). The command Landing of the scene (a button while a ship is driven near a body it can come down on) lists the markers of the body (Markers of its Surface or of its Planet: [{ID, Name, Latitude, Longitude}]) and lets a point of it be picked.
