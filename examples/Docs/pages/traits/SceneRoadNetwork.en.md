# SceneRoadNetwork

The roads of a Scene: an object of kind road (the trait Road: Type of the SceneRoads Service, Points [[x, z] or [x, z, y]] through which it goes, Closed, Width, Height over its line; Bridges and Tunnels [[from, to]] in metres along it, or Structures: true to find them where it goes Clear metres over the ground or Cover under it; Barriers; Along: lines of things beside it) is drawn as its model, and the ground is made level under it, cut where it goes through a hill and filled where it crosses a hollow: a point with no height of its own takes the one of the ground there, and the road goes smoothly between them. A bridge stands on piers over the ground as it is, a tunnel goes under it between its portals. Roads that cross or end on one another meet at a junction, found by itself, or an object of kind junction (the trait Junction: Radius, Surface) at its X and Y; the roads come to its level and their paint stops at it. An object of kind barrier (the trait Barrier: Type (guardrail, wall, jersey, tyres, fence), Height, Color, Step) stands along the road its Road names (Side, Gap, From, To) or on Points of its own (Closed), apart from any road. SurfaceAt(x, z) tells what is under a point, a road, a junction or the ground: {Road (its ID, empty on the ground), Strip, Surface, Height, Grip, Drag}; Roads() gives the roads as SceneRoads makes them, Junctions() the junctions, for what follows them (a car, a train, a walker on a path); BarrierHit(x, z, fromX, fromZ) whether a point went through a barrier of a road. The streets of the settlements of its sketch (their plans, SceneTerrainGenerator.SettlementPlan) are roads too, made with the ground at once: a city or a town has streets, a village country roads, a hamlet gravel, a fort alleys, as wide as the plan has them; each is drawn by an object of kind road of its own (street-<settlement>-<n>), none for a settlement of Roads false.

## Methods

- `SurfaceAt(x, z)`
- `Roads()`
- `Junctions()`
