# SceneNebula

SceneNebula: the clouds of gas and dust of space, each an object of the kind nebula (trait Nebula) a Scene draws as a volume: a ray through its ball, from outside or from within, the light it gives added, what is behind dimmed as far as its dust hides it (the blend premultiplied of Renderer).

- Nebula: Type (hii: a cloud where stars are born, red of hydrogen at its edges, blue-green in its hot heart, torn by dark lanes; planetary: the shell a dying star puffed off, a ring in two lobes; remnant: what a supernova threw out, a thin shell of threads, of its Subtype: Ia round and even, red and white; II torn and lopsided, green, red and blue; Ib and Ic in knots with no hydrogen, blue-green and violet; dark: dust that hides what is behind it), Radius (light years), Seed, Density (how thick its gas and dust are, 1), Glow (how bright, 1), Colors ('#outer, #inner': its own), Blueprint (its look as a graph of the kind nebula of SceneBlueprints, run each frame: it breathes, turns, spreads, its tints, how torn its shape is; Scene.LibraryNebula() the one of the library), Parameters (of its graph).

Its shape is made once into a block of 128³ (64³ at low quality; its slices side by side in one texture, of its Type, Subtype, Seed, Density and the shape of its graph), a band of rows a frame (a stage of the loading: nebulae), and read along the rays at every frame: the noise is not worked out again for each step of each pixel. Seen near, a fine grain of its own over it (Detail of its graph), the steps of each pixel begun a little apart (no rings). NebulaLook(id) gives what it is drawn with ({Type, Subtype, Colors, Dust, Glow, Turn, Grow, Detail, Shape, Side, Baked}); its Info (Describe, the card) its kind and its size; the inspector of SceneEditor offers its Type and Subtype to choose (engine.Options).
