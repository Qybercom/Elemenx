# EditorPanes

EditorPanes: the panes of an editor as wide as the reader drags them (*resizable on their edge), kept in the browser for the next time, each editor by its PanesKey. PaneWidth(name, fallback) is the width of a pane in CSS ('220px'), PaneResized(name, event) keeps the width a drag of its edge ended at (the resizeend of *resizable).

## Methods

- `PaneWidth(name, fallback)`
- `PaneResized(name, event)`
