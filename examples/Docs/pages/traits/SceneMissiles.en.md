# SceneMissiles

SceneMissiles: what guides itself to what it was fired at, and what a ship does against it. A missile is a weapon type (SceneWeapons) of Kind projectile with Guidance pn (proportional navigation: it turns as the line to its target turns, Navigation times, 4, and meets it where it will be, not where it is): Speed (m/s as it leaves its rail), Accel (m/s² of its motor), Top (m/s), Turn (m/s² it can turn by), Fuel (seconds its motor burns and it steers), Seeker (degrees round its nose it sees: lost out of them half a second, it flies on blind), Fuse (metres from its target it bursts), Decoy (metres from a flare it may take it, 4000), Health (what point defence takes of it), Lock {Time, Cone, Count} (how long a ship holds a target in the Cone of its nose, or as its target, to lock it; how many at once), Salvo (missiles a launch, over the targets locked) and Interval (seconds between them). Built in: ship-missile (fast: 300 km/s, through point defence before it fires), ship-hunter (Values.Hunters, Launch Kind hunter, key Y: 25 km/s, heavier; point defence and flares work against it), ship-torpedo (Values.Torpedoes: slow, heavy, its blast wide, point defence can bring it down; an agent launches it at what has Health 500 and more), and of point defence ship-pd. Guidance lead (ship-missile) is simpler: its nose turns at TurnRate (radians a second, 4) to where its way meets the target as that goes now, at any speed, and back to it when it passed it.

A ship carries them as Values.Missiles (the name of the type), Values.Flares (how many, or {Count, Salvo (2 a release), Seconds (4 each burns), Lure (0 to 1, 0.15: how likely a missile that sees one goes to it; it holds to it while it burns, past it it looks again for its target), Speed (m/s they are thrown at, 60), Every (seconds between releases, 1)}) and Values.Defence (the weapon of its point defence, or {Weapon, On}: it fires by itself at the missiles of others that come at it or near, each shot taking Damage of a missile times how likely it hits, Accuracy, 0.35, less the farther it is). A ship locks what stays in the cone of its nose (the ship of the hero: in the middle of the view) and its target (SetTarget of SceneTactics: a lock of its sensors, wherever it is); an agent (SceneAgents) launches at its enemy locked, and against what comes at it releases flares and breaks away.

Launch(id, options) launches a salvo of a ship ({Target, Targets, Count, Kind: missile or torpedo}; else at what it locked): how many go. Driver(id, target) fires its mass driver (Values.Driver, ship-massdriver: a slug of 20 km/s) at target (else its target, else what it locked) where the slug meets it as it goes: true if it fired. Flares(id) releases flares: how many. Defence(id, on) turns its point defence on or off (true or false; none: as it is). Incoming(id) are the missiles at an object [{ID, Source, Weapon, At, Distance (m), Seconds}]. ArmsOf(id) is what a ship has: {Missiles, Left, Magazine, Reloading, Locks [{ID, Progress, Locked}], Locked, Flares {Left, Count}, Defence {Weapon, On}, Incoming, Soonest (seconds), LockedBy, Flying, Torpedoes and Driver {Name, Left, Magazine, Reloading, Ready, Flying}}. An empty id is the ship of the hero (the one it is, sits in or walks in). The keys: M launches missiles, K a torpedo, U fires the mass driver, N releases flares. Events: missile ({ID, Weapon, Source, Target, State: launch, decoyed, reacquired, lost, intercepted (By), hit, spent}), flares ({ID, Count, Left}), and lock and unlock of the ship of the hero ({ID, Weapon, By}). Contacts (SceneTactics) know the missiles and the flares round a ship (Kind missile, flare; Incoming).
