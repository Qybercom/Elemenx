# ElemenxCommon

What needs no page: algorithms, data, numbers, the same code in a page, in a worker and in Node. In Node, `require('@qybercom/elemenx/libraries/ElemenxCommon/node.js')` loads the framework and every Service of the Library and returns `Elemenx` (its Components and Directives, which a page needs, stay out). Its phrases are under `common.*`.

Back to [the Libraries](../../../docs/LIBRARIES.md).

## Services

- `Math`: the numbers of space, for what draws and what computes: matrices of 4 by 4 as arrays of 16 numbers column by column (`Identity()`, `Multiply(a, b)`, `Invert(m)`, `Translation(x, y, z)`, `Rotation(axis, angle)`, `Scaling(x, y, z)`, `Compose(position, rotation, scale)`, `Perspective(fov, aspect, near, far)`, `Orthographic(left, right, bottom, top, near, far)`, `LookAt(eye, target, up)`, `NormalMatrix(m)`, `Apply(m, point)`), vectors (`Vectors.Add`, `Subtract`, `Scale`, `Dot`, `Cross`, `Length`, `Normalize`, `Lerp`, `Distance`), `Hit(ray, center, radius)` (how far a ray meets a ball), `Noise(seed)` (`Value(x, y, z)`, `Fbm(x, y, z, octaves)`, `Ridge`; without a seed the very noise of the shaders of `Renderer`), `SeedOffset(seed)` and `Source(names)` for a worker.
- `Physics`: how things move, the same in a page, a worker and Node: `Steps(clock, elapsed, length, each)` (fixed steps), `Blend`, `Spring`, a car (`Car`, `Gearbox`), `Hit` and `Bounce` of a body on a surface, a ship (`Ship`), a world of bodies (`World(settings)`), `Cloth`, `Rope`, `Waves` and `Fluid`; its vectors and matrices are of `Math`.
- `Random`: random numbers of a seed, always the same for the same seed (a map made again, a replay), or of the time without one. `Create(seed)` returns a generator: `Next()` (0 to 1), `Int(min, max)` (both in), `Float(min, max)`, `Chance(probability)`, `Pick(list)`, `Weighted(list, weight)` (a function of an item or the name of its field, `Weight` by default), `Shuffle(list)` (a new list), `Fork(name)` (a generator of its own for a part, the same for the same seed and name); `Hash(text)` a number of 32 bits; `Noise(seed, size)` a smooth noise of a plane, `noise(x, y)` 0 to 1 (`size` the cells of one wave, 16). `Suprematist` draws with it.
- `Pathfinding`: the ways across a map. `Grid(width, height, cost)` a grid of cells of a cost to enter (1; Infinity blocks), or `Grid(rows, legend)` of rows of text (`.` 1, `#` blocked, a digit its cost, `legend` `{char: cost}`): `Get`, `Set`, `Block`, `Free`, `Passable`, `Clone`. `Path(grid, from, to, options)` the cheapest way (A*), `[{X, Y}]` or null: `Diagonal` (true), `Corners` (false: no diagonal past a blocked side), `Nearest` (to the reachable cell nearest the goal), `Limit`. `Distances(grid, targets, options)` the cost of every cell to the nearest target (Dijkstra); `Flow(grid, targets, options)` a flow field for many units at once: `Next(x, y)`, `Direction(x, y)`; `Search({Start, Goal, Key, Neighbors, Heuristic, Limit})` A* on a graph of your own (`{Path, Cost}`); `Smooth(grid, path)` its corners only.
- `Visibility`: what can be seen on a grid (`opaque` a function `(x, y)` or a grid of `Pathfinding`, its blocked cells hiding). `Line(x0, y0, x1, y1)` the cells of a straight line, `Sight(opaque, x0, y0, x1, y1)` whether one cell sees the other, `Field(opaque, x, y, radius)` the field of view (shadowcasting: `{Cells, Count, Has(x, y)}`), `Fog(width, height)` the fog of war: each cell unknown (0), explored (1) or seen now (2), `Get`, `Seen`, `Explored`, `Show(field)`, `Hide()` (seen back to explored, before the fields of a new frame), `Reveal(opaque, x, y, radius)`, `Reset()`.
- `Workers`: work of the Services of the library off the main thread, in workers made of the very source of their methods (no file to load): `Pool(name, {Service: [members]}, size)` (made once for a name; `Run(service, method, args, callback(error, result))`, the lists of numbers of a result moved, not copied; `Waiting()`, `Inline()`: true with no worker, the runs go at once in the page; `Destroy()`), `Source(owner, names)`: what a Service gives of itself by a method `Source(names)` (`Renderer` and `SceneTerrainGenerator` have it). `Scene` makes the squares of its ground and of the surfaces of planets with them, and the grid of its grass.
- `Astronomy`: the numbers of a star system, as a game needs them, in Node too. Distances in AU, times in days, masses of stars in Suns and of planets in Earths, radii of stars in Suns and of planets in km, angles in degrees, the space of the ecliptic (Z to its north). `Length(value)` (a number in AU, or a text with its unit: `'384400 km'`, `'2.5 Mkm'`, `'5.2 AU'`, `'4.2 ly'`, `'1.3 pc'`), `Period(distance, mass)`, `Position(orbit, time, mass)` (`[x, y, z]` in AU at a day by the laws of Kepler; an orbit is `{Distance, Eccentricity, Inclination, Node, Periapsis, Anomaly, Period}`), `Path(orbit, count)`, `Star(star)` (completed as a star of the main sequence: `Mass`, `Radius`, `Luminosity`, `Temperature`, `Class`, `Color`, `Habitable` `{Inner, Outer}`, `Frost`), `Describe(body, context)` (`Radius`, `Mass`, `Gravity`, `Escape`, `Density`, `Temperature`, `Period`, `Day`, `Habitable`), `Color(temperature)`, `SceneKinds`, `Generate(seed, options)` (a whole system of a seed, its planets by their distance, with moons, rings and belts; `Name`, `Planets`, `Moons`, `Belts`).
- `Diff`: the differences between two texts (Myers: the shortest list of changes). `Lines(before, after)` returns `[{Type: same, remove or add, Text, Old, New}]` (the numbers of the line in each text), `Words(before, after)` the ranges that differ in two versions of a line (`{Before, After}`), `Hunks(lines, context)` the changes with their context and the unchanged runs between them (`{Type: change or fold, Lines}`), `Stats(lines)` `{Added, Removed}`. CodeEditor shows them in its diff view.
- `Formulas`: `MathML(text, display)`, a formula written as in LaTeX as the MathML the browser draws: numbers, letters, the words of functions, operators and relations, Greek letters, groups, powers and indices, `\frac`, `\sqrt` (with `[n]`), sums, products and integrals with their limits, `\lim`, `\left`/`\right`, accents (`\hat`, `\vec`, `\bar`...), `\text`, `\mathbf`, `\mathbb`, spaces and the matrices (`matrix`, `pmatrix`, `bmatrix`, `vmatrix`, `cases`); a text it cannot read comes back in an `<merror>`.
- `DataFormats`: the formats of data and of files, read and written by name: `json`, `jsonc`, `yaml` (YAML 1.2 of the core schema: anchors, merges, block scalars, several documents), `csv`, `tsv`, `ini`, `toml`, `xml` (elements as `{Name, Attributes, Children}`), `query` and `base64`; `Register(name, {Parse, Stringify, Extensions, Mime})` adds formats of an Application, `For(file)` finds the one of a file name or a MIME type. An error names its `Line` and `Column`.
- `Signatures`: how much a signature looks like another or like the samples of a person, a help for an interface and not a check of security (a check that matters runs on a server, the same code in Node). A signature is its strokes `[[{X, Y, P, T}]]` (as `SignaturePad` gives them), compared without its place and its size. `Compare(a, b)` `{Score, Parts: {Shape, Strokes, Proportions, Dynamics}}` (`Dynamics` null without `T`; b may be a reference: the best of its samples), `Enroll(samples)` a reference `{Samples, Mean, Spread, Threshold}` (the score a signature of the person should reach, from how alike its samples are), `Match(signature, reference, threshold)` with `Match` and `Threshold`, `Normalize(signature)`. `SignaturePad` takes a `Reference` (a signature, several, or a reference), `Threshold` and `ShowResult`, emits `compare` after each stroke, and has `Compare(reference)`.
- `Calculations`: formulas of numbers read without `eval` (a page whose CSP forbids it runs them too; the same code in Node). `Evaluate(text, variables, options)` the value (`Price * Count * (1 - Discount / 100)` with `{Price: 12, Count: 3, Discount: 10}`), `Compile(text, options)` a function of the variables read once, with its `Names`, `Names(text)` the names a formula reads. Numbers, names, `+ - * / %`, `^` or `**` (from the right, tighter than a sign before it), comparisons (1 or 0), `&& || !`, `a ? b : c`, parentheses; the functions abs, round(x, digits), floor, ceil, trunc, sign, min, max, sum, avg, sqrt, cbrt, pow, exp, ln, log, the functions of angles and their inverses, atan2, hypot, clamp, if(condition, then, else), factorial, percent(x, of); pi and e. `Angle` degrees or radians, `Functions` of your own. A mistake throws a `SyntaxError` with its `Index`; an unknown name an `Error`.
- `FPS`: how smoothly a page draws, with WebGL or without. `Watch(callback(stats), every)` times the frames of the window by `requestAnimationFrame` while someone watches (returns `{Stop()}`), `Stats()` gives `{FPS, Frame, P95, Max, Slow, Long, Frames, Heap (MB of scripts, Chrome), Sources}` of the last `Window` frames (240), `History()` the times between them, `Report(source, values)` the numbers of a frame of what draws (each `Scene` in sight tells, under a name of its own from `Name(base)`: `Scene`, `Scene 2`..., `Cpu`, `Gpu` by a timer query of the card, `Quiet` and `Skipped`), `Watching()`, `Forget(source)` (a scene that is gone forgets its own).
  - The numbers are of the page: the service of every application reads and adds to the same ones. The component `FPSMeter` shows it (`Corner`, `Graph`, `Sources`, `Every`); the UI kit puts it over every demo with `?fps=1`, and lets the scenes of the demos far from the view go (kept by `Save` with the hero and how it moves, made again when they come near).
  - A `Scene` out of sight sleeps: nothing moves nor is drawn, and its time does not run until it is seen again. A `Scene` draws what moves together of a model of a file that plays no clips as one for each material: all that stands still, each wheel, each side of an opening or a lamp (a car of 1796 meshes is 47 draws); `Merge: false` in the recipe of the model keeps its pieces.
  - What nothing was drawn of for ten seconds goes with its mesh (a car changed for another).
- `Syntax`: the highlighting of source code. `Tokens(text, language)` returns `[{Type, Text}]` covering the text, `Lines(text, language)` the same split into lines, `HTML(text, language)` escaped markup with a `span` of class `elemenx-syntax-<Type>` for each token (the colors are the `--elemenx-syntax-*` tokens of the theme). Languages: `javascript` (`js`, `jsx`), `typescript` (`ts`), `json`, `html` (`xml`, `svg`; its scripts, styles and `{{ }}` in their languages), `css` (`scss`, `less`), `markdown` (`md`; a fenced block in the language it names), `sql`, `python` (`py`), `shell` (`sh`, `bash`) and `text`. `Register(name, {Aliases, Rules, Comment, Indent})` adds a language: `Rules` are `[Type, RegExp, Options]`, the nearest match wins, `Options` read a match in another language (`Inside`) or give types to its groups (`Groups`); `Comment` (`{Line: '//'}` or `{Block: ['<!--', '-->']}`) and `Indent` (a RegExp of the text before a line break that indents the next line) serve an editor. `Find(name)`, `Languages()`.
- `SpatialIndex`: where things are on a plane, found fast (a grid of buckets). `Create({Cell})` returns an index (`Cell` 64, about the size of the things): `Set(id, x, y, width, height)` puts a thing in or moves it (0 by 0: a point), `Remove(id)`, `Has(id)`, `Get(id)`, `Count()`, `Clear()`, `Each(callback)`, `Rect(x, y, width, height, filter)` the IDs that touch a rectangle (a selection frame), `Radius(x, y, radius, filter)` the IDs within a radius, the nearest first (the range of a tower), `Nearest(x, y, max, filter)` `{ID, Distance}` or null; `Distance(x, y, thing)` from a point to a thing.
- `Loop`: the loop of a game or of any world. `Create({Step, Update, Render, Speed, MaxSteps, Window})`: `Update(step, loop)` for every `Step` of the time of the game (1000 / 60 ms) whatever the speed of the screen, `Render(alpha, loop)` once a frame (`alpha` the part of a step past the last update, to draw between two states); `Start()`, `Stop()`, `Pause()`, `Resume()`, `Advance(elapsed)` (time by hand: a test), `Running`, `Paused`, `Time`, `Ticks`, `Fps`, `Speed`, `MaxSteps` (5: a tab back from the background does not run its lost minutes). `Window` gives the frames (the window by default); in Node or a worker the loop runs on timers.
- `Seeded`: what is made of a seed, saved and made again: `Save(kind, params, {Baked, Made, Side})` gives a record `{Kind, Version, Seed, Params}`, its recipe, and with `Baked` the data made too (lists of numbers packed as text: JSON); `Load(record)` makes it again, or reads what was baked; an unknown kind or a newer `Version` is an error. `Define(kind, {Version, Make(params), Bake(made, options), Read(baked, params)})` adds one, `Kinds()`, `Pack(value)` and `Unpack(value)`. Its own kind is `system` (a star system of `Astronomy`); the Services of the other Libraries define theirs as they are made and declare them in `$Provides` (`{Seeded: {Kinds: ['terrain']}}`), so a record of a kind not known yet makes its Service: `terrain` (`SceneTerrainGenerator`; baked: its heights, read by `SceneTerrainGenerator.FromGrid`), `village` (`SceneBiomes`), `model` (`SceneModels`, a model of code; baked: its pieces), `sigil` (`Sigils`), `suprematism` (`Suprematist`; baked: its SVG). Node too.
- `Blueprints`: graphs of blocks kept in the JSON of [Flowprint](https://github.com/Qybercom/Flowprint) with its keys as they are (`blocks`, `links`, `properties: [{key, value}]`, pins `{id, content, direction: {in, out}, place, kind}`) and a few more of the same kind: `version`, `type` of the graph and of each block, `key` and `type` of a pin, `parameters: [{key, type, value, min, max}]`.
  - A file of Flowprint without them reads as version 1. `Read(json)` and `Write(graph)`; `Create(type)`, `Add(graph, type, {properties})`, `Link(graph, [block, key], [block, key])`, `Remove(graph, id)`; `Check(graph)` lists what is wrong (unknown types, pins that are not there, two links to one input, circles of values, types that do not go together, a graph with no end); `Order(graph)`.
  - `Compile(graph)` makes it once per `Hash` (where blocks stand does not count): a `generator` is code of numbers (`Run(parameters, inputs)`, with the noise of `Math`) and a `flow` events and actions: `Start(graph, context)` gives `{Emit(name, payload, callback), On(name, handler), Variables, Stop()}`, with `Actions` and `Timer` of the context; more than 100000 steps at once is an error.
  - The kinds of shaders are of `BlueprintShaders` (ElemenxUI), made as a graph of them or one of their blocks is first met (it declares them in `$Provides`, as any Service that adds kinds and blocks does): a `pass` is a fragment shader (`Fragment`, `Uniforms`, `Textures`, `Values(parameters, {Time, Pixel, Textures})`: parameters are uniforms, so changing one compiles nothing), a `material` the GLSL function `blueprintSurface(position, normal, uv, inout color, alpha, rough, metal, emission, bent)`, a `render` graph passes run in order (`Run(context, parameters)`, each block's `Pass` giving its textures, `Last` the pass that reads a texture last).
  - `Bake(graph)` keeps a pass or a material made, read back by `Compile` without its blocks. `Node(type, definition)` and `Graph(type, {Compile, End, Uses, Bake, Unbake})` add types; `Nodes(graph)`, `NodeOf(type)`, `Graphs()`.
  - Node too.
- `InputDevices`: what the player presses, leans and moves, from every device at once (in Node and a worker nothing is held): keys (`Down(code)`, `Keys()`; a window that loses the focus lets them go), the pointer (`Pointer()` `{X, Y, Buttons, Locked}`, `Moved()` how far since the last call, `Lock(element)`, `Unlock()`), the fingers (`Touches()`), the gamepads (`Pads()`, `Kind()`, `Label(button, kind)`, `Axes()` `{LeftX, LeftY, RightX, RightY, LeftTrigger, RightTrigger}`, `Virtual(name, {Navigate})` a pad of the page: `Press`, `Release`, `Stick`, `Trigger`, `Destroy`), the motion of the device (`MotionSupported()`, `RequestMotion(callback)`, `StartMotion()`, `StopMotion()`, `Motion()`, `Tilt()` `{Steer, Pitch, X, Y, Up}`, `Center()`), and actions: `Bind(action, sources, {Context})` (key codes, buttons of a pad, halves of axes `LeftX+`, `LeftY-`, `RightTrigger`, `Mouse0`, halves of the tilt `Steer+`), `Unbind`, `Value(name)` (0 to 1), `Held(name)`, `Axis(negative, positive)`, `Context(name)` (a binding of another context does nothing), `Actions()`. Events `connect`, `disconnect`, `change`, `button` (`{Button, Pad, Quiet}`), `orientation`, `motion`.

- `Achievements`: the achievements of an Application of any kind (a game, a course, a profile, a tour) as data; it shows nothing and keeps nothing (the Application stores `Export()` and gives it back to `Import(data)`). `Define({ID, Title, Text, Icon, Image, Rarity, Points, Group, Goal, Hidden, Secret})` (or a list), `Progress(id, amount)`, `Set(id, value)` (at its `Goal` it unlocks), `Unlock(id)`, `Reset(id)`, `State(id)` (`{ID, Value, Goal, Part, Unlocked, At}`), `List({Group, Unlocked})` (a `Hidden` one only once unlocked, a `Secret` one with its title and text kept back), `Groups()`, `Points()`, `Count()` (`{Unlocked, Total}`), `On(event, callback)` and `Off` (progress, unlock, reset, change). A trigger of a `Scene` unlocks one (its `Achievement`) and has the action `achievement`; `AchievementQueue` of ElemenxUI shows each unlocked, `AchievementList` lists them.

## Directives

Their styles (the handles, the ghost of a drag, the marks of a drop) are of the themes of ElemenxUI.

- `*resizable` adds resize handles (`right`, `left`, `bottom`, `top`, `corner`) with limits and keyboard support.
- `*draggable` and `*droppable` drag items by pointer onto targets that accept their `Type` (`Hold`: on a touch screen a finger scrolls the page and a press held still that many milliseconds takes the item); a target can also take files and text dragged from outside the browser. A target with `Sort` (`vertical`, `horizontal` or `grid`) is an ordered list: a line marks the place of the item and `Index` in the `dropmove` and `dropped` events tells it, so items can be reordered within a list and between lists. `OffsetX` and `OffsetY` in the detail are the point of the item that the pointer holds. A drag selects no text and survives a new rendering of its element.
- `*infinite` asks for more when the end of a list comes near (`*infinite="{Margin, Direction, Busy, Done}"`, on an element that scrolls or in the page): the event `more` (`{Direction}`) once, again when the list grew or the view went away and came back, never while `Busy` or once `Done`; at once when it is too short to scroll. `Direction: 'up'` asks at the start (the older messages of a chat) and keeps the rows in view where they were when rows come above. `InfiniteList` is a list of it: its rows in its slot, a spinner while `Busy`, a word at its end when `Done` (`EndText`), `Height`, `Margin`, `Label`, `role="feed"`. `VirtualList` tells `more` (`{Count}`) when its last rows are drawn, once for each length; `Chat` tells `history` near its first message (`HistoryBusy`, `HistoryDone`).
- `*swipe` makes an element follow a finger along an axis (`*swipe`, `*swipe="'y'"` or `{Axis, Threshold, Mouse}`; the page still scrolls along the other one) and dispatches `swipemove` (`{DX, DY}`) while it moves, then `swipe` (`{Direction, DX, DY}`: left, right, up or down) past `Threshold` pixels or on a flick, or `swipecancel`; a move that begins on a field, a slider or `[data-no-swipe]` is left to it. `Gallery` and the pictures of a `ProductCard` swipe by it, and a menu sheet closes by a swipe down on its top.
- `*mount` puts custom content into an element: a DOM node, or an object with `Render(container, item)`.

## Components

- `VirtualGamepad`: a gamepad on the screen for the fingers (in the slot `hud` of a `Scene`): a stick, a second one with `Right`, `Buttons` (a, b, x, y, lb, rb, lt, rt, start, select), `Show` auto (where the screen is touched), always or never, `Tilt` (its stick across follows the lean of the device as a wheel), `Navigate`, `Size`; a `Virtual` pad of `InputDevices`; event `button` (`{Button, Down}`).
