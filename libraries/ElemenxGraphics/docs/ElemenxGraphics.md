# ElemenxGraphics

What draws a world in three dimensions, on `ElemenxUI`: scenes and their objects, the ground, the sky and the water, the models and their files, the light, roads, triggers, the editors of scenes and models, and the graphs of `Blueprints` made into shaders. Its phrases are under `graphics.*`.

Back to [the Libraries](../../../docs/LIBRARIES.md).

## Services

- `Renderer`: the graphics of three dimensions on WebGL2, with no library (its numbers of space are of `Math`, its shapes of `Meshes`). The camera runs in Node too: `Camera(options)` (`Ortho`: the height of a view in parallel rays; `View()`, `Projection(aspect)`, `Matrix(aspect)`, `Orbit(target, yaw, pitch, distance)`, `Ray(x, y, width, height)`, `Project(point, width, height)`), `CloudBlock(size)` (the block of noise of clouds, RGBA bytes; `Work()` makes it in a worker), the noise of `Math` in GLSL in `Chunks.Noise`, the light of up to 4 points with the shadows of up to 4 balls in `Chunks.Lights`, a shadow map in `Chunks.Shadow`.
  - `Create(canvas, options)` returns a renderer, or null without WebGL2: `Program(vertex, fragment)` (an error of GLSL thrown with its log), `Check(vertex, fragment)` (asks the card at once whether two shaders compile: their logs, or null), `Mesh(geometry)` (`Update`, `Instance(data, count)` for copies drawn at once, `Destroy`), `Lines(points, loop)`, `Points(positions, extra)`, `Texture(source, options)` (`Float` for an array of floats), `Target(width, height, {Colors, Depth, DepthTexture, Float, Samples, Mipmaps})` (textures to draw into, `Begin()`, `End()`), `Viewport(x, y, width, height)`, `Shadow(size)` (a depth texture with `Begin()` and `End()`), `Draw(mesh, program, uniforms, settings)` (`Mode`, `Blend` alpha or add, `Depth` (false, or lequal: drawn again over the same surface), `DepthWrite`, `Cull`, `Instances`), `Winding(clockwise)` (the front of a triangle by the way its corners turn: clockwise for what is drawn through a mirror), `Resize(width, height, ratio)`, `Clear(color)`, `Lost`, `Destroy()`.
- `Meshes`: the shapes of three dimensions made of triangles, in a page, a worker and Node: `{Positions, Normals, UVs, Colors, Indices}` (`Sphere`, `Icosphere`, `Ring`, `Box`, `Cylinder`, `Torus`, `Plane`, `Lathe`, `Wedge`; `Transform(geometry, matrix)`, `Merge(parts)`, `Displace(geometry, amount)`, `Normals(geometry)`, `Facets(geometry)`, `Simplify(geometry, cell)`); `Geometry()` an empty one. What `Renderer` draws and what the models of `SceneModels` are built of (`tools.Meshes` of a recipe).
- `SceneKinds`: the kinds of the objects of a game, of three axes: `Define(name, {Extends, Traits: {Name: params}, Parts: [{ID, Kind, Position, Rotation, Traits}], Values})`, `Get(name)`, `Has(name)`, `Names()`, `Resolve(name)` (made whole: `{Name, Chain, Traits, Parts, Values}`; the later layer wins a value, Traits merge by name and field, Parts by ID, `{ID, Removed: true}` takes one away), `Make(kind, props)` (an object `{ID, Kind, Values, Traits, Parts}`, its parts objects too, `parent/part`), `Field(object, name)` and `Fields(kind)` (a field in any case, or a trait and its field together), `Diff(object)` (what a saved game keeps: the kind and what differs) and `Restore(saved)`. Node too.
- `SceneTerrainGenerator`: the ground of a game, continuous: `Flat(options)` (a flat map of noise: `Size`, `Height`, `Water`, `Scale`, `Ridges`, `Seed`; `Height(x, z)`, `Normal(x, z)`, `Slope(x, z)`, `Walkable(x, z, {MaxSlope, Swim, Blocked})`, `Chunk(x, z, size, resolution)` a geometry with a skirt, `Path({X, Z}, {X, Z}, options)` a way of points `{X, Y, Z}` round what cannot be walked, or null) and `Sphere(options)` (the ground of a planet, the very one its baked surface shows: `Radius`, `Height`, `Seed`, `Ocean`, `Type`; `Land(direction)`, `Elevation(direction)`, `Point(direction)`, `Altitude(point)`, `Patch(face, u, v, size, resolution)`). Node too. Off the main thread: `Work()` (its workers), `Piece(shape, options, roads, args)` (a square of `Flat` or `Sphere` of their options, as `Chunk` and `Patch` make it, in lists of `Float32Array` and `Uint32Array`), `Grid(options, side, top, steep)` (the heights of a flat ground over all its size, and where grass grows); a field keeps its `Options`. `Heights(field, side)` (its heights in a grid, a `Float32Array`) and `FromGrid(options, heights, side)` (a field of such a grid: baked or painted; its `Grid` and `Side`).
- `ModelFormats`: the files of models read and written in one form, the asset of `Gltf`: `Formats()` (`glb`, `gltf`, `fbx`, `obj`), `Detect(data, name)`, `Read(data, {Format, Name, Buffers, Materials})`, `Parse(data, callback, options)`, `Load(url, callback)` (with what a file names beside it: the buffers of a .gltf, the mtl of an obj, pictures), `Write(asset, format, options)` (`glb` and `fbx` an ArrayBuffer, `fbx` with `{Text: true}` and `gltf`, `obj`, `mtl` text), `FromModel(model)` (a model of `SceneModels`, of code with its clips, as an asset to write). `SceneModels` loads every format of it. `Fbx` reads FBX binary and text in full (`Read(data)`: nodes with their pivots and pre-rotations, meshes by material, Phong made PBR, pictures inside or beside, skins, clips; `Write(asset, {Text})`; `Tree`, `Inflate`), `Gltf.Write(asset, {Text})` writes glTF. Node too.
- `BlueprintShaders`: the kinds of graphs of `Blueprints` made into shaders, defined as it is made (Blueprints makes it when it meets one of them: nothing to call first): `pass`, `material` and `render`, the blocks `UV`, `Pixel`, `Position`, `Normal`, `Texture`, `Output`, `Surface` and `Present`, and the GLSL of the blocks of numbers of Blueprints, which then stand in its graphs too.
  - A pass and a material are baked (`Blueprints.Bake`). Node too. Shaders written by hand go where a graph goes: `Blueprints.Compile({type: 'material' or 'pass', glsl})`, and the `Blueprint` of a material, a ground or a `Shader` pass of a pipeline takes the text of GLSL itself or the URL of a `.glsl` file (drawn once it has come).
  - A material is its `blueprintSurface` and what it calls, a pass its `main` (the head added) or a whole fragment shader (`#version`); `uniform` lines are read: `uParameter_<Key>` a parameter (its value by `// = 0.5`), a `sampler2D` a texture (`uTexture_<Name>`), and `// needs: Noise` puts those parts of the shaders in.
  - The block `GLSL` is code written in a graph of shaders: `Code` (lines of GLSL in a scope of their own, its inputs there as variables, its outputs given by setting them; a `uniform` line declares one), its pins named by its properties `Inputs` and `Outputs` (`Tint: vec3, Gain: float`; the pins and their links follow when they change), `Needs`; an error of its code is told on the block: `Errors(made, log)` gives `[{Block, Line, Message}]` of the log of a shader that does not compile.
  - The kind `vertex` moves the corners of a shape: `blueprintVertex(position, normal, uv, sway, phase, size, inout world, inout bent)`, of blocks (`World` where the corner is, `Position`, `Normal`, `UV`, `Sway`, `Phase` and `Size` of the copy, `Wind` the gust of the scene, the numbers, `GLSL`, ending at `Vertex`) or written by hand; the `Vertex` of a material of a `Scene` (a graph, GLSL or a `.glsl` file, with the `Parameters` of the material) moves it in every pass that draws it, its shadows too.
  - The wind of a `Scene` is a vertex graph too: `WindGraph` (a graph, its JSON or its GLSL) in place of the one of the library, `LibraryWind()` (what sways bends by its `Sway` and `Size`, each copy at its `Phase`, the gust moving it round), in the shaders of copies, of skeletons and of what is sown; the `Vertex` of a material runs after it, and a model of `Sway: 0` does not sway.
  - The grass leans by a vertex graph of its own: `GrassGraph` in place of `LibraryGrass()` (the wave over the field, the lean of each blade, its stir near the eye; `Position` the place of a blade and how far up it a corner is, `Normal` the way it faces, `UV` how near the eye, `Phase` its seed, `Size` how tall; the wave in X of the `Normal` of its end lightens the crests).
  - The sky and the land are graphs too, each whole: `SkyGraph` (kind `sky`, `blueprintSky(inout color, inout alpha)`) in place of `LibrarySky()` (its blocks `SkyRay`, `SkyDay`, `SkyAir`, `SkyDusk`, `SkyHalo`, `SkyPink`, `SkyWisps`, `SkyCover`, `SkyEclipse`, `SkyTowns`, `SkyRainbow`, `SkyMoonHaze`, `SkyMoon`, `SkyCompose`, the end `SkyEnd`) and `LandGraph` (kind `land`, `blueprintLand(inout color, inout alpha)`) in place of `LibraryLand()` (`LandPoint`, `LandGrain`, `LandPools`, `LandAlbedo`, `LandPaint` (the `Blueprint` of the ground over it), `LandLight` (the sun with its shadows, the sky, the bounce, the lamps), `LandSheen`, `LandCompose`, the end `LandEnd`); each block is the very GLSL of the shader it came from, so the library draws as it did, and a graph of its own may take any part (numbers and `GLSL` blocks stand in them too).
  - A graph (or GLSL written by hand) made into a shader that does not compile is told once by the event `shader` of the scene (`{Kind (material, vertex, land, water, wind, grass, effect, pass), Owner, Errors: [{Block, Line, Message}], Message}`, each error on the block of GLSL it is in) and the console, and what the library has is drawn in its place; `SceneEditor` marks the errors on the blocks of the graph open.
- `SceneBlueprints`: what a `Scene` adds to `Blueprints`, declared in `$Provides` (Blueprints makes it as a graph of an effect or one of these blocks is met): the kind `effect` (the layers of an effect, ending at `Effect`; `Make(parameters)` of what `Compile` gives is the type of the effect) with its blocks `Layer` and `Effect`, and the blocks of the passes of the picture of a `render` graph: `Scene`, `Clouds`, `Occlusion`, `Air`, `Frames`, `Mirror`, `Rays`, `Exposure`, `Bokeh`, `Bloom`, `Picture` and `Shader`, each running the step of the scene that runs it.
  - And the kind `water`: the water of a `Scene` as a graph, `blueprintWater(inout color, inout alpha)` in its shader of water, of blocks that read what that shader has (`Place`, `View`, `Far`, `Flow` with its `Speed` and `Way`, `Sun`, `Time`) and the parts of the water of the library, each its very GLSL: `Waves` (the slope of a lake, or of a river drawn out along it and carried down it, with `Rush` where it runs white), `WaterNormal`, `Fresnel`, `Glints`, `Sky`, `Body` (the color of the depth, with the silt of a river), `Foam`, `Reflection` (what is round it, mirrored on the screen) and `WaterFog`, ending at `Water`; the numbers and `GLSL` stand there too.
  - `Scene.LibraryWater()` is the water of the library made of them, and the `WaterGraph` of a scene (a graph, its JSON or its GLSL) takes its place.
- `SceneModels`: the models of a game made of code, about 1 tall, trees of pieces that move on their pivots: units (`knight`, `archer`, `mage`, `orc`, `villager`, `soldier`, `robot`, `mech`), animals (`wolf`, `deer`), vehicles (`tank`, `rover`, `drone`), plants (`pine`, `oak`, `birch`, `palm`, `bush`, `stump`, `mushroom`), rocks (`rock`, `boulder`, `crystal`), buildings (`house`, `tower`, `wall`, `castle`, `barracks`, `windmill`, `hangar`, `dome`, `reactor`, `radar`, `silo`, `gun`) and ships (`fighter`, `cruiser`, `freighter`).
  - `Names({Group, Type})`, `Has(name)`, `Info(name)`, `Define(name, {Group, Type, Size, Speed, Variants, Sway, Matte, Smooth, Clips, Build(tools, random)})` or a file, `Define(name, {URL, Type, Size, Speed, Clips: {walk: 'Run'}})` (idle, walk, run, attack, die and work are found by the names of its animations), `Load(name, callback)`, `Ready(name)`, `Bake(model)`, `Make(name, {Variant})` (`{Name, Key, Size, Speed, Sway, Matte, Clips, Pieces: [{ID, Parent, Pivot, Team, Geometry}]}`), `Whole(model)`, `Bounds(geometry)`, `Levels(name, {Variant})` (simpler geometries for what is far), `Pose(model, clip, time, {From, FromTime, Fade})` (the matrix of each piece), `Length(model, clip)`.
  - A clip is `{Length, Loop, Hold, Stride, Tracks: {piece: [[time, rx, ry, rz, mx, my, mz]...]}, Spins: {piece: [x, y, z]}}`. Node too.
- `Gltf`: glTF 2.0 and GLB, the models other programs make (Blender and the rest export them; FBX and OBJ come through `ModelFormats`). `Parse(data, callback, {Buffers})`, `Load(url, callback)` (the file, the files it names and its pictures), `Write(asset, {Text})` (a .glb, or a .gltf with its buffer inside), `Meshopt(mode, source, count, stride, filter)`. Reads meshes, materials of metal and roughness with their maps, skins, animations, `KHR_mesh_quantization`, `KHR_texture_transform`, `KHR_materials_unlit`, `KHR_materials_specular` and `EXT_meshopt_compression`; a file that requires another extension (Draco, KTX2) is refused with its name. Node too, but for its pictures.
- `SceneBiomes`: the lands of a game: `Names()`, `Has(name)`, `Get(name)`, `Define(name, {Ground, Scatter, Grass})`, with `temperate`, `taiga`, `tundra`, `desert`, `savanna`, `swamp` and `alien`; `Village(field, {X, Y, Houses, Radius, Seed, Shapes})` lays out houses and roads on what is level and dry. Node too.
- `SceneRoadGenerator`: roads of a seed: `Track(seed, {Length, Corners, Wild, Type, Barrier, Gap})` a closed race track, `{Seed, Spec (the road for a `Scene`), Length, Start: {X, Z, Heading, At}}`.
- `SceneRoads`: the roads of a scene, of any kind, as a line and a cross-section: surfaces (`Surface(name, {Color, Rough, Metal, Grip, Drag})`: asphalt, concrete, cobbles, paving, gravel, dirt, sand, grass, ballast, runoff, rumble, cycle, steel, wood) and types (`Define(name, {Title, Group, Speed, Strips: [{Kind, Width, Surface, Height, Way, Left, Right, Marking}], Rails: [{Offset, Gauge}], Sleepers: {Step, Length, Surface}, Catenary})`, `Type(name)`, `Types()`; declared in `$Provides` as `{SceneRoads: {Types: [...]}}`): motorway, highway, road, country, lane, street, avenue, alley, boulevard, tram, dirt, gravel, path, footpath, cycle, sand, track, rally, kart, drag, runway, taxiway, rail, rail-double, rail-narrow, rail-wide, rail-yard, platform.
  - Optional `Barriers` of a road (`[{Side, Kind (guardrail, wall, jersey, tyres, fence), Height, Gap, From, To, Color, Step}]`, `BarrierHit(roads, x, z, fromX, fromZ, hints)`; `FreeBarrier({ID, Points, Closed, Kind, Height, Color, Step, Smooth}, options)` one on a line of its own), `Bridges` and `Tunnels` (`Spans`, `SpanAt(road, metres)`), `Along` (lines of things beside it: rows of lamps, fences, hedges), `Marks` (start, line, stop), the wires of a railway or a tram (`Catenary`), and networks (`Network(specs, junctions, options)`: the junctions where roads cross or end on one another, the roads brought to their level, `JunctionAt(junctions, x, z)`).
  - `Line(points, {Closed, Step, Height, Straight, Segments, Radii})` makes a line through its points and even: each part between two points (`Segments` of a road: `line`, `arc` (`{Kind: 'arc', Bulge}`: how far its middle stands off the straight line, to the left above 0) or `curve`, smooth, the default; `Straight` makes them all straight), each corner between two straight parts rounded by its `Radii` (an arc tangent to both) (`[{X, Y, Z, Way, Along, Bend}]`), `Road({ID, Type, Points, Closed, Width, Height})` a road of it with its strips at their offsets, `Near(road, x, z, hint)` where a point is on it (`{Offset, Along, At, Way, Y}`), `Under(roads, x, z, hints)` what is under a point (`{Road, Strip, Surface, Height, Grip, Drag, Offset, Along}`, the higher road where two lie over each other, null off them), `Sketch(roads, junctions)` the lines a ground is made level under and the lines of things beside them, `Asset(roads, {Height, Junctions})` their model for `SceneModels.Define(name, {URL: name, Asset, Fit: false})`: the strips, the sides of what is raised, the markings, the rails and the sleepers.

## Components

- A land in one attribute: `<SceneObject Kind="ground" Biome="taiga" />` brings the colors of a ground, what grows on it and its grass; `<SceneObject Kind="village" X="0" Y="0" Houses="10" />` lays out houses and roads there, and nothing is sown on them.
- What fills a ground in masses, in the same `Scene`: a `SceneObject` of a kind with the trait `Scatter` (`forest`, `grove`, `thicket`, `rocks` or one of your own) sows models by its rules: `Shapes`, `Density` (a hundred square units), `Size`, `MinHeight`, `MaxHeight`, `MaxSlope`, `Cover`, `Patch`, `Clumps` (thick in groves, thin between them), `Seed`, `Blocks`; of a kind with the trait `Grass` (`grass`: `Spread`, how unlike in height the blades are, a lawn 0.05, a wild meadow 0.6; `Clumps` and `Patch`, tufts and glades) it grows blades in the vertex shader round the view: `Density`, `Height`, `Color`, `Root`, `MaxHeight`, `MaxSlope`. There is no object for each thing: they live in squares of the ground, made a few a frame, drawn whole near, in simpler levels farther and as a picture baked from all sides far away, each fading into the next; the streams are made again only when the view moved. `Sown()` tells `{Things, Squares, Waiting, Draws, Blades}`. What stands still and has no clips is placed once and kept in buffers, so an ordinary object costs nothing while it stands.
- Models from files, in the same `Scene`: `Model` on a `SceneObject` names a model of the `SceneModels` Service or a file (`Model="assets/hero.glb"`); it is fetched once, stands on the ground 1 tall as the others are, `Size` scales it, its animations are its clips (`Clip`, `Play`, walking on a way), all its copies are one draw with their frames read from a texture of its skeleton, its materials are lit by metal and roughness. `Soft` (0 to 1, or `{material: value}` in `Define`) lets light under a surface, as in skin. `Shapes` of a `Scatter` takes files too: near they keep their pictures, far they take the mean color of them. The files go in the public folders of the project, the build copies them, and the percent of a splash counts them.
- The light of a `Scene` (the trait `Lighting`): it draws into a texture of floats and makes the picture of it: the air between the eye and what it sees, rays of the sun, ambient occlusion, bloom and an ACES curve, frames that add up (TAA) and water that mirrors what is on the screen; two shadow maps of soft edges; the light that bounces off what the sun sees into shadows and walls (one bounce, near the view); leaves and blades that let the sun through. `Quality` (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `ultra`; empty chooses by the graphics card and steps down while frames are late); `Graphics()` tells what is on. A scene out of view draws nothing, an expensive frame leaves the page as much time after it, and a machine with no graphics card gets `low`.
- A `Scene` takes behaviours: what a component inside it (a game: `GameStrategy`, `GameRace`) or code adds to what it does, of which the scene knows nothing. `AddBehaviour({Start(scene), Step(elapsed, now), Pointer(kind, event), Key(event), KeyUp(event), Stop()})` returns the function that detaches it; the scene starts it once it runs (at once when it runs already), steps it every frame before it is drawn, gives it a press first (true takes it from the scene: kind `down`, `move`, `up`, `wheel` or `double`), and stops it as it goes. A component of the trait `SceneBehaviour` declared inside a `Scene` attaches itself so, its methods `SceneStart`, `SceneStep`, `ScenePointer`, `SceneKey`, `SceneKeyUp` and `SceneStop` the hooks; `ParentScene()` is the scene.
- A `Scene` has a sky of its own: kind `sun` with `Time`, `DaySeconds`, `DayHours`, `Latitude`, `Tilt`, `DayOfYear`, `YearDays`, the moon at night (`Moon`, `MoonLight`, `MoonSize`, `MoonPhase`, `MoonElevation`, `MoonAzimuth`, `MoonTilt`, `MoonNode`, `NightLight`, the light of towns `SkyGlow`, `SkyGlowColor`, `SkyGlowAzimuth`; stars turn with the planet, a moon before the sun or in the shadow of the planet is an eclipse), `Cosmos` of a scene (`nebula`, `pillars`, `galaxy`, `galaxies`, `cluster`, `void`, or `{Kind, Seed, Colors, Gas, Dust, Stars}`) makes the far sky of a seed; `Galaxy` of a sun is its band over a night.
  - `Weather` (`clear`, `cloudy`, `overcast`, `rain`, `storm`, `snow`, `fog`, `changing`) with `Front` (the seconds of a change), `Wind`, `WindWay`, `Flare`, `Exposure` (a number holds it, empty adapts as an eye does), `Bokeh` (0 to 1: the depth of field, how wide the lens is open; a bright point out of focus becomes a disc) and `BokehFocus` (the distance in focus; empty: the hero, or the middle of the view, followed slowly).
  - The passes that make the picture of a frame are a render graph of `Blueprints`: `PipelineGraph()` gives the one of the library (Scene, Clouds, Occlusion, Air, Frames, Mirror, Rays, Exposure, Bokeh, Bloom with its `Threshold`, Picture, Present), and `Pipeline` takes a graph of its own in its place: a block switched off is left out (its Color goes on), a `Shader` block is a pass graph of its own (its `Graph`, at its `Scale`, given `Color` and `Depth` as textures, its parameters from `Parameters` and `PipelineParameters`) before the Picture or after it; a graph that cannot be made is told once and the library's is drawn.
  - A ground takes a `Sketch` (`SceneTerrainGenerator`): layers of strokes (raise, lower, flatten, smooth), lines (ridge, valley, canyon, river with its water flowing downhill, as fast as it falls or as its `Flow` says, road, cliff), areas (plateau, basin, lake with its water, terraces, swamp, beach, floodplain, dunes, erosion, biome, mask that freezes what it holds) and circles (mountain, crater, stamp) over its noise, in the page and in its workers alike; a change of it makes the ground again only where it changed, the squares there drawn as they were until their new ones come.
  - Its sketch places things too, sown by the `Scatter` of the scene: areas of growth (forest, meadow, bushes, thicket, jungle, sequoia: `Density`, `Shapes`, `Passability` from 0, none goes through, to 1), clearings and masks where nothing grows (nor on roads, rivers and lakes), fields and orchards in rows (`Spacing`, `Gap`, `Way`), lines of things a step apart turned along their way (row, fence, hedge, wall, powerline: a fence, a hedge or a wall as long as its step and in the way of walkers), and markers, objects of kind `marker` (`Role` spawn, camp with its tent and fire, ruins, cave, spring, `Team`, `Name`) a game finds by `Objects('Marker')`; `SownNear(x, z, radius)` tells what is sown round a point.
  - A `settlement` of a sketch (`Kind` hamlet, village, town, city or fort, `Density`, `Block`, `Street`, `Walls`, `Lit`, `Seed`) is laid out by `SceneTerrainGenerator.SettlementPlan`: its streets (the `street` lines drawn in it, else a grid along its longest side or a main street with lanes off it, none on its edge) made roads, its lots along them facing their street, wholly inside it, off its `square` areas, the streets and each other, each a building of its `district` (`Kind` residential, market, craft, noble, business, industry, fields: wheat in rows) or of its kind, towers and business centres in the core of a city; a fort walled round with a tower at each corner and a gate on the wall where each street goes out; `citywall` lines and `landmark` circles stand on their own; the plans are `Settlements()` of the sketch of a ground.
  - Each building is a model of the `SceneBuildingGenerator` Service, of any size from a shack to a business centre: `Classes()`, `Options({Class, Floors, FloorHeight, Width, Depth, Roof, Walls, Podium, Setbacks, Crown, Balconies, Lit...})`, `Random(options, seed)` (the same seed the same building), `Define(name, options)` (a recipe of `SceneModels` as tall as its `Size`, with its tiers, windows lit as `Lit` tells, balconies, roof and crown).
  - New models: sequoia, jungle, fern, reed, wheat, vine, fence, hedge, stone-wall, pole, tent, ruin, city-wall, wall-tower, gate, well, lighthouse, temple. The places of a scene where something happens, in a game, a tour or any other world, are triggers (the trait `SceneTriggers`): kind `trigger` (`Shape` circle with its `Radius` round its X and Y, area of `Points`, line of `Points` and `Width`; `Height` over the ground, 0 any; `Team`, `SceneKinds` of what counts in place of the walkers, vehicles and hero, their bases too; `Once`, `Enabled`, `Achievement`: the ID of an achievement of the `Achievements` Service it unlocks as one comes in, or `{ID, Amount}` it brings on) makes the scene emit `enter` and `leave` (out or removed) with `{Trigger, ID, Object}`, where `Trigger` is the trigger itself, `Trigger(id)` of the scene (`ID`, `Inside()`: who stays in it, `Action(name, data)`, `Enable(on)`): a handler sends it an action and the scene emits `action` (`{Trigger, Name, Data}`); a trigger with `Blueprint` (a flow graph) runs it, its `Event` blocks enter, leave and those of its actions, its `Action` blocks the functions `TriggerActions({Name: function (value, done)})` gave, and the action `achievement` there by itself.
  - Kind `zone` (`Points` or a `Radius`, `Tags`, `Team`) is a place of rules: `ZonesAt(x, z)` gives `[{ID, Name, Tags, Team}]` (safe, pvp, no-build or any tag). A sketch draws them too: `trigger` (an area), `triggerpoint`, `triggerline`, `zone`, which change no ground.
  - `SceneEditor` edits a scene as Unreal edits a level: the view flown with the right button and W A S D Q E, a gizmo that moves, turns (`Facing`) and scales, the fields of what is selected and the parameters of its `Blueprint`, its graph under the view, T the roads (a road of the type chosen drawn point by point, a press again on its last point or Enter ends it, Escape lets it go; its parts straight, arcs or smooth, its corners rounded by the radius chosen; the road selected shows its points to drag, Delete takes the one chosen away and its corner radius is among its fields, and the middles of its parts: a press goes from straight to an arc to smooth, a drag bends an arc or adds a point), B a sketch over its ground (its type, its layers, its items and their fields), Play and Stop, the document in the browser or a JSON file; `Scene` gives it `Viewpoint()`, `SetViewpoint(view)`, `Project(point)`, `Ray`, `GroundAt`, `Pick(x, y, true)` and the values `Facing` and `Lift` of what stands.
  - `Setting="space"` makes a new document a star system (a star and a planet; a scene with no ground is edited so): its `SpaceKinds` added round the selected body or the one that fits (a planet round the star, a moon, a ring or a station round a planet), each an orbit farther out; the gizmo carries a body on its orbit in the plane of its parent (its `Distance` and `Anomaly`, `OrbitAt(id, clientX, clientY)` of the scene), tilts its orbit on the green axis, turns its axis with the ring and scales its `Radius`; Day draws the system at a day of it, and Ctrl+D copies a body a little farther on its orbit.
