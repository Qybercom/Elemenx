# ElemenxGaming

Back to [the Libraries](../../../docs/LIBRARIES.md).

## Components

What makes a scene a game, on `ElemenxGraphics`. Its phrases are under `gaming.*`.

- The windows of a game, on ElemenxUI (their phrases are under `gaming.ui.*`; their look and the themes `fantasy` and `scifi` of the game look are in `index.css` and `themes/` of this Library): `GameHud` (the scene in its default slot and nine areas over it: `top-start`, `top`, `top-end`, `start`, `center`, `end`, `bottom-start`, `bottom`, `bottom-end`; the areas let the pointer through to the scene), `GameBar` (`Value` of `Max`, `Kind` health, mana, experience, stamina or any name with its token, `Segments`, `Size`; a loss leaves a trail that follows a moment later, a low bar pulses), `GameSlot` (an action of a hotbar: `Icon` or `Image`, `Label`, `Key` bound through the Hotkeys Service, `Count`, `Rarity`, `Cooldown` in milliseconds with a sweep and the seconds left, `Cool(ms)` from the code, `Active`, `Color` (the glass under its icon), `Badge`, `Classes`, `Data` (what it holds: an item or a skill), `Group`; the pointer or the focus shows the card of its `Data` when it has more than a name (`Description`, `Type`, `Rarity`, `Stats`), as an inventory does; it is dragged and takes what is dropped on it, `drop` with `{Item, From, To, Current}`; events `use`, `ready`, `drop`; several in `<div class="elemenx-game-hotbar">`), `GameInventory` (a grid of `Slots` in `Columns`, `Items` by cell with `Name`, `Icon` or `Image`, `Count`, `Stack`, `Rarity` (the frame of its cell), `Color`, `Badge` (a mark in a corner: `+5`), `Class` (more classes of its cell and card, for a look of its own), `Active` (an item in use, as one worn: the active look of the theme), `Type`, `Description`, `Stats`; a card for the item under the pointer or the focus, fixed to the viewport so a window that scrolls does not cut it; the arrows move, Enter or a double press uses, Space picks an item up and puts it down, Delete discards, items lie on glass of their `Color` or of their rarity; events `move` with the new `Items` for the owner to keep, `receive` (`{Item, From, To, Current}`: something dropped from elsewhere), `give`, `use`, `discard`, `select`).
  - The pointer drags the things of a game everywhere: every cell of an inventory and every slot is both the place a drag starts and a place it ends, with one Type, `game-item`, and Data `{Item, From}` (`From` is the place: `{Kind: 'inventory', ID, Name, Index}` or `{Kind: 'slot', Key, Name}`); any `*droppable` of the game that takes `game-item` joins in.
  - A drop is a move: the owner applies `GameItems.Move`, so a thing leaves the place it came from, piles join and the rest swaps, `GameDialogue` (`Speaker`, `Portrait`, `Text` typed at `Speed` characters a second, `Choices`; a press shows the rest, digits choose; events `next`, `choose`; the keys it takes do not reach the shortcuts of the page), `GameItemCard` (the card of an item: `Item`, its name in the color of its rarity, its type, stats and description; `Place`, a style fixed to the viewport that `GameItems.Place(element)` gives beside an element; the inventories and slots show it, and any place of the items of a game may), `GameMenu` (`Title`, `Subtitle`, `Items`; the arrows go round, Escape emits `back`, `select`), `GameKey` (the prompt of a control: the button of the pad in use, `Button` a, b, start..., or the key of the keyboard, `Keyboard`, with a `Label`; the button drawn as on the pad of its kind (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, or the places of a generic pad), and `Pad` shows the buttons of one kind whatever is plugged in), `GameTabs` (tabs numbered 01, 02... in a column or a row: `Items`, `Value`, the `change` event; the arrows, Home and End), `GameQuests` (the quests: `Items` `[{ID, Title, Group, Level, Status (active, done, failed), Tracked, Text, Objectives [{Label, Value, Max}], Rewards [{Icon, Image, Label, Count, Rarity, Color}]}]`; `Variant` `log`, the journal with the quests by group (the arrows, Home and End) and the open one (`Value`) with its objectives, their progress and its rewards, and a button that tracks it, or `tracker`, the objectives of the tracked active quests for the screen of the game; events `change` (the ID), `track` (`{ID, Tracked}`)), `GameMinimap` (the frame of a map in its default slot, `Shape` round or square, `Size`; `Markers` `[{ID, X, Y, Kind (quest, enemy, ally, point), Icon, Label, Color}]` at X and Y from 0 to 1, one off the map waits on its edge pointing the way; the player's arrow turned to `Heading`, `North`, `Title` and `Note` under the map, `Zoomable`; events `zoom` (1 or -1), `marker`), `GameLeaderboard` (`Items` `[{ID, Name, Score, Rank, Avatar, Icon, Note, Change, Me}]` by score or by Rank, medals for the first three (`--elemenx-game-medal-*`), `Change` the places risen or fallen, `Limit` (10) with the player of the page (`Me`) shown apart below it, `ScoreLabel`, `Selectable` and the `select` event).
  - A game window is a `Window` with the class `game`: `<Window class="game">` is a main window of the theme, `class="game secondary"` a smaller one, `class="game plain"` a dialog or a message.
  - For the content of game windows: `.elemenx-game-panel` (the panel of the theme on any element), `.elemenx-game-section` with `.elemenx-game-section-title` (a section on a band), `.elemenx-game-separator` (a line with the ornament of the theme, on an `hr`), `.elemenx-game-stats` (a `dl` of names and values; `double` for two columns) and `.elemenx-game-good`, `.elemenx-game-bad`, `.elemenx-game-special` (the colors of values); buttons and fields inside a game panel or window take the look of the theme.
  - Their look is the `--elemenx-game-*` tokens (see `THEMING.md`): by default the colors of the page. The theme `fantasy` makes the windows of a classic MMO (clear glass with a warm inner glow in a thin light frame and a shadow under its text, a header drawn as a picture with scrolls at both ends and a crest over the centered title, buttons whose frame turns into brackets at the corners and leaves the sides open, items on a quiet glass of their color in a frame of their rarity, gauges whose track and rounded caps make one vessel in a ring of metal; dark only); `scifi` the light lines of a digital frontier (clear black glass, a thin line of cyan light with two corners cut at 45 degrees, crosses on the corners, sections with their title set into their frame, stats in pairs of cells, gauges as a dim haze over a bright line with a white end, cyan for the light and orange for a threat, windows that open as a projection; dark only).
  - A game changes any token on its own.
- `GameRace`: a race on a closed road of a `Scene`, declared inside it (`<Scene> ... <GameRace Road="track" Laps="3" /> </Scene>`, the trait `SceneBehaviour`): `Road` (its ID; empty: the first closed road), `Laps`, `Racers` (the IDs of the vehicles; empty: the one under the keys), `Checkpoints` (how many a lap has, evenly round the road: a lap counts only through all of them, so a way back over the line counts nothing), `Countdown` (seconds), `Auto` (starts with the scene). `Start()`, `Stop()`, `Standings()` (`[{ID, Position, Lap, Progress, Time, Last, Best, Finished, Wrong}]`). Events: `countdown` (`{Seconds}`), `start`, `checkpoint` (`{ID, Lap, Index}`), `lap` (`{ID, Lap, Time, Best}`), `position` (`{ID, Position}`), `wrong` (`{ID, Wrong}`: the wrong way, or the right one again), `finish` (`{ID, Position, Time, Best}`), `end`.
- `GameStrategy`: a strategy game on the ground of a `Scene`, declared inside it (`<Scene> ... <GameStrategy Team="blue" Fog="true" /> </Scene>`, the trait `SceneBehaviour`): the walkers of its `Team` are the units of the player; a press selects one (Shift adds or takes it away), a frame drawn with the left button selects all in it, a double press all of its kind on the screen, Ctrl+A all of them, Escape none; a right press on the land sends them there, each to its own spot (`Spread` metres apart), their ways round the water and the slopes; on a unit of another team they attack it, S stops them. A unit with `Damage` (per second) attacks what is within its `Range`, going to it first, and by itself what comes within half its `Sight`; one with no `Health` left is gone. `Fog` hides what the units do not see and darkens what they have not seen yet or see no more, the units of other teams hidden there. `Selected`: the units selected from the start. `Selection()`, `Select(ids)`, `Order(ids, target, kind)`, `Stop(ids)`, `Seen(x, z)`, `Explored(x, z)`. Events: `select` (`{IDs}`), `order` (`{Kind, IDs, X, Z, Target}`), `death` (`{ID, By}`).

## Services

- `GameItems`: the rules of moving the items of a game. `Move(item, current)` returns `{To, From}`: what a place holds after `item` comes onto it (`current` is what it held) and what goes back to where the item came from; piles of the same `ID` join up to `Stack`, other items swap. `Stacks(a, b)`, `Take(item, count)` (an item used up: `null` when none is left), `Tip(item)` (what `*tip` takes to show the card of an item beside an element, `GameItemCard`; none for an item with only a name) and `Carded(item)` (whether an item has more to show than its name). `GameInventory` moves inside its grid with it, and the owner of the other places of a game does the same with what their drop events carry. `GameInventory`, `GameSlot` and the rewards of `GameQuests` show the card of an item by it.
